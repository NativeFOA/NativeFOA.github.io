Abstract

NativeFOA is a unified framework for spatial audio representation, instruction-guided editing, and evaluation directly in the first-order Ambisonics domain. It combines a spatially faithful latent representation with conditional flow matching to support trajectory modification, source removal, and source replacement while preserving the surrounding sound field.

Three editing tasks

01

Move

Relocate a target source along a specified trajectory and within an optional time interval.

02

Remove

Selectively eliminate a semantic or spatially specified target while retaining the interferer.

03

Replace

Insert new source content while inheriting the target's spatial and acoustic attributes.
